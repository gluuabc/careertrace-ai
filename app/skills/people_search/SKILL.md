# People Search

Determine person type and target organization, school, or research topic. Search
existing user connections and permitted public sources. Preserve identity and
source evidence, and never infer private contact information. Recruiters require
public evidence of a current recruiting or talent-acquisition role at the target
organization.

Read `source_policy.md` and `identity_rules.md` when source or identity ambiguity
matters.
